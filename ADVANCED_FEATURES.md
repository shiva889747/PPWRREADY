# Advanced Features Added

1. **Edit / Delete Records** — manage the packaging register as a real master-data application.
2. **Bulk CSV Import** — validates required columns, previews data, then imports confirmed rows.
3. **JSON Export + Summary API** — demonstrates machine-readable interoperability.
4. **Supplier Analytics** — groups records by supplier and compares readiness and evidence coverage.
5. **Remediation Workflow** — creates owned actions with due date, priority and status.
6. **What-if Simulator** — estimates portfolio readiness after hypothetical gap closure.
7. **Assessment History** — saves point-in-time snapshots for trend tracking.
8. **Audit Trail** — logs create/update/delete/import events.
9. **Data Dictionary** — documents business meaning and assessment role of each field.
10. **Improved Record View** — includes management actions and edit/delete controls.
11. **Management Report** — summarises portfolio readiness, findings and actions.
12. **Offline-first UI** — no external CSS/JS framework is required to run the demo.
